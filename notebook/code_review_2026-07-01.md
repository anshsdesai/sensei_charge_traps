## 1. Findings, severity-ranked

### F1 [HIGH — confirmed by independent adversarial review] Same-step self-recapture applies to all traps; physically it belongs only to the V3-phase subset

`fast_readout_numba` (ccd_simulation.py:802-827) processes each per-row step as *emission first*
(occupied trap emits into the packet that just shifted into the trap row), *then* a capture check on
that same packet. Every trap therefore has a same-transfer-window self-recapture channel (verified
by micro-test: emit_p=1, α→∞ ⇒ the carrier never escapes).

Phase geometry: during a transfer the packet crosses V3 of its row, then V1 of the next row. For a
**V3 trap** the packet crosses the trap *on exit, after* receiving the dwell's emission — the code's
ordering is right. For a **V1 trap** the packet crossed the trap *on entry, before* the emission —
an emitted carrier exits without recrossing, so it should escape with no same-step recapture (unless
one argues the emitted carrier preferentially joins the *upstream* packet, which restores a
one-step-delayed channel). If the catalog is a V1/V3 mix, the code overestimates the futile
self-recapture cancellation for the V1 subset.

Why it matters: the instrumented budget (physics.md §4.3) shows self-recapture is ~91% of the
readout emission flux and the masked observable is the ~0.3% residual of these flows — the ordering
is load-bearing for the *escaped-emission* term. Mitigation: the masked net was measured
binning-invariant and capture-limited, which suggests the observable is dominated by the capture
side, not the emission side; but that was measured *under this ordering*.

**Independent review verdict (see §4 for the full text):** the phase-geometry reasoning is
confirmed, and the one candidate rescue — that a carrier emitted from a V1 trap joins the *upstream*
packet, restoring a delayed self-recapture channel — **fails on electrostatics**: during the dwell
the carrier sits between the ~200 kT V3 barrier upstream and the V2 well of its own pixel
downstream, so it joins the packet that already crossed the trap with essentially probability 1.
Emissions during the ~100 µs transfer windows are a ~2×10⁻⁶ fraction. The reviewer's quantitative
assessment: per-emission escape for the large-α V1 subset goes from e^{−α} (≈0.09 for the
futile-cycling tail) to 1; the net sign on the 61-count masked residual is not determinable
analytically (competing terms: more escaped emission (+) vs shorter displacement/lower V1 occupancy
(−)), with plausible O(1) fractional impact; and the **upper-limit slope component is plausibly
biased low — anti-conservative** — because the τ ∈ [3e5, 5e7] s drivers correlate with large σ
(hence large α) and their occupancy is insensitive to the fix while their escaped-emission rate is
not.

**Recommendation (agreed by both passes):** add a per-trap phase flag (Bernoulli ½ V1/V3); for V1
traps do the capture check on `q` *before* the emission roll (and give no same-step check to the
emitted carrier). ~10-line kernel change + an A/B campaign at the central scenario to measure the
delta before quoting the UL slope. A V1/V3 phase fraction other than ½ can be carried as a knob.
Also add this asymmetry to physics.qmd §6's known-simplifications list until fixed.

### F3 [MEDIUM, documentation — still present in quarto] The production finder is product-rule, not "separate-lobe 3σ"

`dipole_algorithm.qmd:134-135` (unchanged from the md) states the chosen operating point requires
"both lobes … **separately** significant at 3σ (product-only rules … are not production-admissible)".
The live minimal finder
(`dipole_new.findDipoles2`, dipole_new.py:118-155, called with `robust_sigma=True,
symmetry_perc=None` from run_charge_traps.py:257) applies **only** the product test
`image[r]·image[r−1] < −(3σ_MAD)²` plus the repeat-appearance filter — a 1σ×10σ pair passes. The
9,333-candidate catalog was produced by this finder (confirmed on disk), which is a third

separate-2.5σ). Downstream cuts do the real rejection and the trap-free null test covers the full
chain, so this is a documentation error, not a results error — but the note's claim is exactly
backwards vs. the shipped code, and the finder-completeness numbers quoted (91%) belong to a
configuration that is not the one in production.

### F4 [LOW-MEDIUM — still present in quarto] The ×0.96 energy-fit haircut is not applied in the completeness curve that builds the simulation seed populations

The notes' convention (completeness_efficiency.qmd §5/§9) is "completeness = WS1-corrected
intensity reach × 0.96". In code, the 0.96 is deliberately *not* baked in
(`characterization_probability.py:710-725`, `APPLY_SURVIVAL_K=False`, haircut "reported
separately") — and `figure_utils.plot_upper_limit_hist` / `plot_efficiency_corrected_hist`
(figure_utils.py:1186-1330) divide by `default_curve` = the un-haircut intensity reach. The
UL/efficiency-corrected seed histograms (17,475 / 14,305 traps) are therefore ~4% *smaller* than the
stated convention implies (anti-conservative, though well inside the quoted ~25% Poisson band).
Similarly the stage-10 statements ("0.992 @ 10³ s") are reach, not reach×0.96.
**Fix:** one deliberate line — either multiply `default_curve` by 0.96 when seeding, or state
explicitly that seeds use the un-haircut reach and the 4% is carried in the error budget.

## 4. Independent adversarial review (merged)

A fresh agent (no access to this review's conclusions beyond the framed questions) independently
re-derived the kernel physics. Where we overlap we agree; it contributed three findings the inline
pass had not fully developed. Its severity list, merged with mine:

**F1 (Q1) — CONFIRMED, upgraded to HIGH.** See F1 above; the rescue argument is closed off
electrostatically, the UL-slope direction is anti-conservative, and the fix + A/B is spelled out.

**F8 [MEDIUM, new] `drain_traps_empty_numba` is inconsistent with the code's own recapture model
for V3 traps.** The 3 h drain uses P_emit = 1−e^{−T/τ} (zero recapture in the empty tail). That is
exactly right for V1 traps — but under the code's *own* readout/clear physics, a V3 trap's emitted
carrier joins the passing empty packet (q=1) and gets a recapture check at 1−e^{−α} per emission,
independent of shift speed. The self-consistent V3 closed form is **P_drain = 1−exp(−T·e^{−α}/τ)**
(thinned emission). Consequence: a large-α V3 trap that survives `fast_clear_numba` *because* of
cycling is then drained recapture-free by the very next call — `three_hour` mode over-drains the
large-σ V3 reservoir, contaminating the clear-mode (G) axis comparisons. (For the τ ≫ 3 h UL traps
e^{−3h/τ} ≈ 1 either way, so physics.qmd §4.4 finding 2 survives.) The CLAUDE.md line "in the
fast-shift limit recapture is negligible" is false for large-α V3 traps. Fix rides along with the
F1 phase-split patch.

**F9 [MEDIUM-LOW, new-ish] Clear-boundary deletion of emitted charge is non-conserving —
quantify it.** `simulate_clear` ends with `ccd_state.fill(0.0)` (ccd_simulation.py:1308), deleting
carriers emitted into the last ≤512 packets of the clear while their traps are marked empty.
