1. Where this sits in the pipeline

Dipole detection (finder) → per-dipole intensity(tph) fit at each temperature → per-trap SRH E/σ fit across temperatures → τ(135 K) histogram → Method-3 simulation completeness. This tab covers the first three (detection + selection + characterization), which together produce the τ(135 K) catalog the Method 3 Completeness tab consumes.

Two code paths, selected by run_charge_traps.py --pipeline {legacy,minimal}: legacy dipole.py (git HEAD) and minimal dipole_new.py — the synthesis used going forward. Minimal artifacts are suffixed _minimal / _caldet so the two never overwrite each other.

2. What the minimal dipole finding does

The minimal pipeline keeps the legacy peaked-emission physics (and the SRH prefactor) but makes the measurement honest about sign, baseline, and per-point noise. Five deliberate changes:

AspectLegacyMinimal (forward)Why
Dipole intensityabsolute=True — |I| folded positive absolute=False — signed I=(lobea−lobeb)/2 Sign encodes trap orientation; needed to detect sign-flip contamination
Baselinethrough-zero (no offset)fitted constant pedestal offset Data plateau at ≠0 at high T (readout deferral); through-zero biases τ
Per-point errorspatial 34×34 patch σ (error_model='patch', ~183 e−) temporal pair-noise (error_model='physical', ~36 e− base; absolute_sigma=True) Patch σ overestimates the per-point error ~5×
Finderhistogram-σ threshold + 0.3 symmetry cut robust_sigma=True (MAD), symmetry_perc=None Histogram σ is inflated by dark-current tails at high T → desensitized
Orientationnonesign-consistency class gates the SRH fit A single real trap pumps one direction at all T

Selection then gates on the across-temperature SRH fit with a dof-insensitive reduced χ² < 4 (this replaced a biased p-value cut — see the Validation & Fixes tab §1).

3. The signed + pedestal intensity model

Both pipelines use the identical peaked model (dipole_new.py:477); the minimal one adds the offset:

I(t_ph) = 3000 · coeff · (exp(−t_ph/τ) − exp(−8 t_ph/τ))  [+ offset]
  • The bump is 0 at t→0 and 0 at t→∞, peaking at t* = ln(8)/7·τ ≈ 0.30τ, height ≈ 1950·coeff. Every curve starts and asymptotes at offset.
  • Negative intensities are real: signed dipoles with the opposite orientation. coeff and offset are both signed and need not agree (offset orientation is set by readout direction).
  • Different start points = the per-temperature pedestal (legacy forced everything through 0).
  • Monotonic-looking curves at extreme T = the bump apex sits off-screen (very cold τ>window: rising edge only; very hot τ<window: flat at offset).
The pedestal is physical and load-bearing. It is the readout-deferral (CTI) pedestal: during readout, dark/background charge clocked through the trap's pixel is partly deferred, making a fixed dipole independent of tph. The fitted |offset| tracks dark current and is highly significant:

median |offset| [e−]95105311950895755598

The pumped amplitude is ~T-independent (≈1000 e−), as a fixed-charge-per-cycle deferral should be. Caveat: at 125–130 K (τ ≫ window) offset and coeff are partially degenerate — treat cold-tail offsets with care.

4. Selection criteria & funnel

A trap enters the catalog iff WellBehavedTrap (≥4 good temps) & OrientationConsistent & GoodEnergyFit & not EnergyFitFailed. The per-temperature intensity detection uses a calibrated Δχ² guard (0.1%/temp); the per-temperature intensity GOF is well-calibrated (kept good-temp reduced χ²: median 0.90, IQR 0.65–1.17).

5. Purity — false-positive rate

Run through 8,000 trap-free control pairs, the full minimal selection yields 1 false positive → catalog FPR ≈ 0.01% (~0.4 expected noise false positives in 2,928). Purity is enforced upstream by the calibrated per-temperature detection guard, the ≥4-temperature requirement, and sign consistency; the SRH χ² cut's real job is rejecting blends (real charge, wrong model), which a trap-free null cannot measure. Full null test in Validation & Fixes §3.

1. What Method 3 computes

Method 3 is a data-driven measurement-completeness for the catalog produced in the Detection tab. For a clean single trap at a point in the (τ135, E) plane it estimates the probability that the experiment would have detected and characterized it, and decomposes the misses into (a) the trap never entering the measurement window and (b) selection inefficiency. The headline quantity is P(≥4 good intensity fits | τ135, E) — the intensity-reach — multiplied by the energy-fit factor (§4).

2. How it builds the completeness map

Stage order (run_chain.py): 01 records → 02 noise parity → 03 noise map → 04 intensity-error scaling → 05 amplitude prior → 06 single-curve recovery → 07 single-temperature pdet → 08 forward pdet grid → 09 characterization probability → 10 completeness.

Stage 08 — the forward detection grid

For each grid point (temperature T, emission time τ, amplitude A) it runs an injection-recovery: build the noisy intensity-vs-delay curve, fit it with the flavor's intensity model, apply the per-temperature cuts, and record the pass fraction as p_det(T, τ, A). The injection is now flavor-consistent: minimal scatters with the calibrated pair-noise floor (~36 e−) and a realistic pedestal drawn from the catalog into the truth; legacy uses its patch σ and reconstructs signed→abs (see Validation & Fixes §5 for the as-built injection).

Stage 09/10 — from pdet to P(characterized)

For each (τ135, E): infer log σ from the SRH relation, compute the implied τ(T) at every measured temperature, interpolate the stage-08 grid at A from the stage-05 depth prior, then form the intensity probability = P(≥4 of 23 temperatures detected) via a Poisson-binomial. Out-of-band policy: any temperature whose τ(T) falls outside the stage-08 τ band gets p_det=0; when all temperatures are out of band the trap is unrecoverable by construction (~15% of the grid, beginning at τ135 ≈ 193 s). Stage 10 averages over the observed E distribution to make the completeness curve.

3. τ(135 K) is an extrapolation — reading the map

The map's x-axis is the emission time at 135 K. For a long-τ135 / high-E trap, τ at 135 K is far outside any pump delay — such a trap is not measured at 135 K; its τ135 is the SRH extrapolation. It was actually detected at high temperature, where τ(T) drops into the measurable window.

This is why characterized traps appear in apparently low-probability regions of the completeness map: they are survivors of a population, and the far-right arm was caught hot, in-window. Coloring the characterized traps by the temperature at which they got their good intensity fits makes this explicit — the right arm is hot (~200–250 K) and the gradient tracks the diagonal high-probability ridge (corr(log τ135, mean good-T) = +0.62). See the Figure Reference tab, “completeness map (caught-at-T)”.

4. The energy fit is catalog-derived

Completeness factorizes as intensity reach × energy-fit factor. Crucially, no Method-3 stage runs the SRH energy fit — stages 06–08 forward-model only the per-temperature intensity detection, and stages 01/05/09/10 merely read the catalog's stored GoodEnergyFit/WellBehavedTrap flags. Consequences:

  • The SRH χ² threshold is catalog-only. Changing it (Detection §6) never touches the stage-08 grid. Regenerate the catalog, then run_chain.py --reuse-grid reruns just 01/05/09/10 — no ~24h grid rebuild.
  • Do not confuse it with the intensity-detection Δχ² threshold (delta_chi2_vs_constant), which is baked into stages 06–08 and does require a grid rebuild if changed.
  • The energy-fit factor was measured by a lean injection-recovery that runs the real SRH fit on clean traps (Validation & Fixes §7): for minimal it is ~0.96 with calibrated στ, so P(characterized) ≈ 0.96 × P(≥4 good intensity) — no separate forward grid needed.

5. Flavors: minimal is the headline

Both flavors run through the same chain via --flavor {minimal_caldet, legacy}. The lean check showed minimal's recovered τ errors are calibrated (pull RMS 1.01) while legacy's are ~1.7× optimistic (pull RMS 1.69, up to 2.7 at the cold edge), its SRH survival propped only by the loose reduced χ²<5 gate. Minimal is the pipeline to headline; legacy is a reference comparison. (Details and numbers: Validation & Fixes §7.)

1. Selection: the SRH goodness-of-fit bias & fix

Discovery. The across-temperature SRH/Arrhenius GOF, applied as p>0.05, is statistically biased against well-measured traps. With ~15 precise τ points and tiny errors the χ² test has the power to reject on the documented ~1% high-T Arrhenius lean, so traps lying visually perfectly on the line (e.g. srh_gross_0: 16 temps, p=1e−6) were cut, while kept traps survived mainly by having fewer temperatures.
SRH pass-rate vs # temperatures4–56–78–1011–1415–30
OLD p>0.0565%41%28%21%32%
NEW χ²<475%60%53%60%77%

Of the 2,563 OLD SRH failures: median reduced χ² only 4.25, 29% had reduced χ²<3 (visually fine), and only 19% had reduced χ²>10 (genuinely non-Arrhenius). Fix: a dof-insensitive reduced_chi_squared < srh_reduced_chi2_max (=4.0) at dipole_new.py:810 — it does not get stricter with more points and still removes the reduced χ²>10 blends.

5. Method 3: the noise-injection bug & the flavor-consistent fix (WS1)

Bug (found via a Codex 5.5 stress-test). Method-3 stage 08 injected spatial patch noise (_local_sigma=np.std(patch), median ~183 e−) for both flavors. Correct for legacy (which detected with patch σ) but wrong for minimal, which detected with temporal pair-noise (~36 e−) and a threshold calibrated on it — a ~5× over-noising. It made the old minimal completeness (the ~0.44 peak) far too pessimistic.

Fix (WS1) — flavor-consistent injection in full_pdet_grid_pilot.py (_simulate_grid_point) + the production mirror full_pdet_grid.py. Three decisions resolved against the live code/data:

  1. Minimal shot term is |Itrue|/4, not /2: the live (Sa+Sb)/4 uses max(charge−row_median,0), so the dark dipole pixel clamps to 0 and only the bright pixel survives. Injected σ = sqrt(σbase2(T,q) + |Itrue|/4), σbase from pair_noise_table_minimal.npz (median ~36 e−).
  2. Pedestal injected into the simulated truth for both flavors, drawn from the catalog signed fit_offset pool. Signed median ≈0 but |I0| grows with T (~99 e−@135K → ~1040@170K) — the physical readout-deferral pedestal.
  3. Legacy = signed→abs reconstruction: legacy spectra are absolute=True, so inject the signed truth, add patch noise, then np.abs(), then fit offset-free. This reproduces legacy's real high-T pedestal-driven rejections rather than hiding them (true_offset=0 would have over-claimed legacy completeness).

Consistency check passed: rebuilt minimal injection σ median ~36 e− (matching the calibration scale), not ~183.

8. The two double peaks — diagnosed & resolved

Two curves in this analysis showed a shoulder-dip-peak. They are different objects on different axes with different (both now-understood) origins — do not conflate them.

(A) Completeness vs τ135 — the survival(k) artifact (removed)

Decomposing stage 09: the intensity reach P(≥4 good intensity) is smooth/monotonic; the dip appeared only after multiplying by the empirical survival(k) (legacy, selection_model=None, had no dip). survival(k) is keyed on the good-temperature count alone, so its τ135 projection is non-monotonic — artifactual — and it conflated completeness with purity. Removed in stage 9 (§7); the completeness is now the intensity reach (clean energy-fit cost ~0.96 flat is reported separately; contamination as purity).

(B) Naive efficiency vs τ(T) — the legacy pedestal/GOF collapse (real; fixed by minimal)

The naive measured/(measured+extrapolated) curve vs the emission time at the measurement temperature is a different object. Stage 11 (naive_efficiency_closure.py) forward-models it and attributes the dip:

Predictorreproduces the dip?χ²/bin
stage-8 detection physics (unconditional)no — smooth high plateau6900
+ ≥4 conditioningno (identical to unconditional)6900
× empirical per-temperature GOF survival (hybrid)yes135

So the dip is not intrinsic detectability — it is the per-temperature INTENSITY-fit GOF survival collapsing at high T, projected onto τ(T) by Arrhenius (hot temps → short τ(T) → collapsed GOF → dip). The GOF here is the intensity-curve test p_value > 0.05 (dipole.py:599,611,616; useIntensityErr), not the SRH energy-fit reduced χ² < 5. It collapses because legacy's through-zero model cannot fit the high-T pedestal → large intensity χ² → p<0.05.

Minimal (pedestal-fitted) fills the dip — confirmed on the real catalogs. Per-temperature good-fit rate at high T rises from legacy ~0.16 to minimal ~0.45 (145→210 K); the naive dip lifts 0.13→0.41 (dip/peak 0.15→0.53). The failure mode shifts: legacy high-T failures were p_value (GOF) dominated; minimal's are amplitude_significance<3 dominated (~55%), p_value only ~28% — i.e. the pedestal fix removed the model-misfit collapse.

The residual minimal dip is genuine, not an artifact. At the dip temperatures (170–195 K) the curves are in-window (95–98%) with flat noise (~180 e−), yet ~half fail amplitude_significance<3: the fit cannot establish the bump at 3σ because the offset (pedestal) DOF inflates σcoeff and a faster high-T bump gives less amplitude leverage. That is a principled detection limit (and the legitimate cost of fitting the pedestal), so the residual dip should stay. (Diagnostic: tools/naive_dip_legacy_vs_minimal.py → figures/naive_dip_legacy_vs_minimal.png.)

9. High-T completeness optimism — the forward grid's blind spot

Investigating whether the WS1 completeness models the high-T detection penalty (it injects the pedestal and applies amplitude_significance<3, so it ought to) showed that it does not: the completeness is ~2× optimistic at high T for genuinely good traps.

Leave-one-temperature-out holdout test (the unbiased check; codex5.5 design). Cohort = traps that are bright (amplitude estimated from cold/intermediate good temps 140–165 K only, never the target T), in-window, and grid-easy (stage-8 pdet>0.9). At target T = 175–200 K the grid predicts ~0.95 but the observed good-fit rate is ~0.45 (0.37 at 200 K). Among the cohort misses the controlling cut is p_value-dominated (45–67%), not amplitude_significance — so it is not faintness (and not the amplitude-selection bias of a naive fold, which this test removes). Stage 8 is missing a real high-T failure mode.
